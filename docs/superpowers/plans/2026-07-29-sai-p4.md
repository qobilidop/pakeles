packet-for-packet with the pinned sonic-pins sai parser (e77250b8,

## Corpus matrix (~22 lines)

**Accepts:** eth-only (unknown ethertype), ARP, v4/TCP, v4/UDP,
v4/ICMP, v4/other-proto (accept, no L4), v6/TCP, v6/UDP, v6/ICMPv6,
v6/other-nexthdr (accept), VLAN+v4/TCP, VLAN+v6/UDP, VLAN+ARP,
VLAN+unknown (accept), v4 no-L4-bytes (proto=TCP but truncated after
IP → err PacketTooShort, bitmap has ipv4 not tcp).

**Truncations (laxness → err):** eth 10 bytes, v4 header truncated,
v6 header truncated, TCP truncated after v4, vlan tag truncated, ARP
truncated, v6 truncated after ethertype.

< 14B still injectable to simple_switch (unlike XDP) — include a short
one.
